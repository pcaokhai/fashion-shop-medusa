# VN Commerce Kit: one-pager

**For:** Vietnamese SMEs selling 100–1,000 products online who want a store that looks finished, takes COD and VNPay,
and that the shop owner can run without a developer.

## What the client gets (scope)
- Storefront in Vietnamese: home, category listing with filters, product page, search that ignores accents, cart, single-page guest checkout, confirmation.
- Payments: COD and VNPay (payment confirmed only by the verified server notification).
- Vietnamese addresses (province and ward) at checkout; flat-rate shipping.
- Stock admin (Medusa dashboard): products, stock, orders; confirmation email on every order.
- Hosted demo first, then your brand: colours, fonts and copy are theme variables, not a rewrite.

Not in the first release: guest order lookup, refunds, GHN waybills, VietQR, reviews, flash sale. Each can follow as its own step.

## Why this and not a template
Real Vietnamese payment flow tested end to end against the VNPay sandbox, accent-insensitive search, and a design
system built for Vietnamese text, so diacritics, long product names and 375 px phones are first-class.
